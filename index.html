Server Rules & Guidelines

To keep Kayapata SMP fair and fun for all players, we enforce strict anti-cheat policies. Violations will result in immediate permanent bans.

Strictly Prohibited

  • No Hacks or Hacked Clients: Any form of fly, killaura, speed, or movement exploits.
  • No ESP Mods: X-Ray, Player ESP, Chest ESP, or Tracers.
  • No Combat Automation: Auto-Totem, Crystal PvP macros, or auto-clickers.
  • No Hardware/Software Macros: Any external keybind automation or auto-pvp scripts.
  • No Hitbox Modifications: Any mod altering entity reach, hitboxes, or hit-detection.

Allowed Clients & Mods

  • Vanilla Clients: Standard Java Minecraft, Bedrock Edition, and official launchers.
  • Performance Optimization: Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, Iris Shaders, and Optifine.
  • Cosmetic PvP Texture Packs: Visual texture adjustments (short swords, low fire, clean GUI) - Provided they contain no hitbox or x-ray overlays.
